WHO ARE CORTIS?

CORTIS is a five-member boy group under BIGHIT MUSIC. The group consists of MARTIN, JAMES, JUHOON, SEONGHYEON and KEONHO.


The name CORTIS comes from the phrase "COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES", representing the group's desire to think differently, experiment freely and create without being limited by conventional boundaries.


CORTIS is known for its collaborative creative approach. Instead of simply performing material created by others, the members participate in songwriting, composition, choreography, video production and visual direction.
